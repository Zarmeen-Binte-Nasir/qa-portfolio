Exploratory testing · Candidate portal · August 2026

EmpRadar candidate applications case study

I tested the candidate-facing application journey across listings, assessments, offers, employment records, bookmarked jobs, and related navigation. Findings were documented with reproducible steps, expected versus actual behavior, severity, status, and evidence.

  • 14areas reviewed
  • 7findings documented
  • 2applications in test data
  • ChromeWindows 10

Scope and approach

Session-based exploratory testing using a candidate account. I compared related screens, exercised available actions, checked empty and populated states, and captured evidence.

Covered

  • Application listing and table state
  • Application details navigation
  • Assessments and screening status
  • Offers and employment records
  • Bookmarked jobs and empty states
  • Candidate history and related navigation

Scope limitation

The available account had the candidate role. Employer-only ATS capabilities—including pipeline boards, moving candidates between stages, shortlisting, rejection, and employer bulk actions—were not functionally tested and are not claimed as defects.

Highlighted findings

Two screening-flow inconsistencies were prioritized for follow-up.

High Screening

Pending assessment count was inconsistent

The dashboard reported one pending assessment, while the Assessments page reported zero pending items and a 100% completion rate.

View evidence →
High Navigation

Assessment call-to-action did not reach the assessment list

Activating the dashboard assessment prompt left the candidate on the dashboard instead of opening the relevant assessment experience.
