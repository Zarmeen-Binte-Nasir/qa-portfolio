How I test

  1. Understand risk What can lose money, data, or trust? Those become P0.
  2. Document then automate Cases and RTM first; automate the stable, high-value paths.
  3. Assert like a product owner Not just “button clicked” — state, messages, APIs, and edge cases.
  4. Report clearly Repro steps, expected vs actual, severity, and a fix suggestion.
